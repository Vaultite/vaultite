# Northfield card index

Cataloguing and studying a scholar's card index held in the Northfield University special collections: roughly 14,000 cards in 31 drawers, left by a historian of the university's founding faculty. Feeds section 5 of [[Draft]] and aim 1 of [[Infrastructures of attention proposal]].

## Status

- Drawers 1 to 12 photographed (Bob Carter, summer 2026)
- Numbering scheme reconstructed for drawers 1 to 6: subject letters, then running numbers, with occasional decimal insertions
- Cross-references found on about one card in nine, mostly by subject and number
- No keyword register found yet; the special collections librarian thinks it may be in an uncatalogued box

## Questions

- Does the scheme look more like Otlet's classification [@otlet1934] or like Luhmann's fixed addresses [@luhmann1981]?
- Which cards are referenced most often, and are they the oldest ones? A small test of cumulative advantage inside one system [@merton1968; @price1965].
- What can't the photographs show: handling wear, inserted slips, the order on the desk [@latour1986]?
