# Knowledge infrastructures workshop, talk notes

Two-day workshop, 2026-05-14 to 2026-05-15. My talk: day one, second session, 25 minutes plus discussion.

## Talk plan

1. Open with the memex desk and the problem of trails [@bush1945] (3 min)
2. The card index as a paper machine [@krajewski2011] (5 min)
3. Luhmann's rules: fixed numbers, branching, links, register [@luhmann1981] (7 min)
4. Extended and distributed cognition: Otto's notebook and the navigation team [@clark1998; @hutchins1995] (5 min)
5. Infrastructure, and why the word matters [@star1999] (5 min)

## Discussion, notes taken after

- Strongest pushback: "infrastructure" risks meaning everything. Answer with Star's properties, one at a time, and show which a private card index has and which it lacks.
- Someone asked about collaborative vaults in labs: shared notes as boundary objects [@star1989]. Worth a section in [[Draft]].
- A historian asked whether Gessner really belongs in the story. Yes, but only through [@blair2010], not as a direct ancestor.
- Good question on cognitive load: does linking every note make writing harder [@sweller1988]? Leave for the teaching material.
