# Panel proposal: paper technologies of knowledge

Organiser: Alice Park. Due 2026-04-01; submitted 2026-03-28. Status: accepted.

## Panel abstract

Historians of science have increasingly treated paper as a technology rather than a support: slips, tables, forms and cards that perform operations on knowledge as well as recording it [@krajewski2011; @blair2010]. This panel brings together four papers on paper technologies between the sixteenth and the twentieth centuries. Together they ask how material formats shaped what counted as a finding, a fact or a field, building on accounts of writing as a technology of the intellect [@goody1977; @ong1982] and on studies of how inscriptions become facts [@latour1986].
