# Paper machines outline

Working outline for [[Draft]]. Target: Journal of Knowledge Practices, special issue on research infrastructures (full paper due 2027-01-31).

## Argument in one line

A note system is infrastructure for thinking: its addressing, linking and retrieval rules decide what a researcher can find again, and so what they can write.

## Sections

1. Introduction: memex to card index, the infrastructure claim [@bush1945; @star1999]
2. Notes before computers: Gessner, commonplace books, Otlet [@blair2010; @krajewski2011; @otlet1934]
3. Luhmann's partner: fixed numbers, branching, links, register [@luhmann1981; @schmidt2016]
4. The extended note: parity principle and distributed cognition [@clark1998; @hutchins1995]
5. From links to citations: hypertext and the citation index [@nelson1965; @garfield1955; @price1965]
6. Conclusion, plus the Northfield card collection (see [[Northfield card index]])

## Open questions

- Is "infrastructure" doing work here, or is "technology" enough? Reviewer at the workshop pushed on this.
- How much of the psychology literature to keep: [@mueller2014] has a mixed replication record, so it should be framed as a caution, not a finding.
- Does Goody's list argument [@goody1977] need Ong as well, or is one enough?
