# Indexing the margins

Early idea for a second paper, after [[Draft]] is out. Not started.

## The idea

Marginalia are the most private layer of a reader's note system and the least studied as a system. Early modern readers annotated with symbols that pointed to their own commonplace headings, which makes the margin an index into another book [@blair2010]. The question is whether the same holds for digital annotation: are highlights and comments an index, or just a residue?

## Possible material

- Annotated copies in the Northfield special collections (ask the curator what is catalogued)
- Exported highlights from a few volunteers' reading apps, with consent
- Library circulation data as a crude proxy for which books get read closely
