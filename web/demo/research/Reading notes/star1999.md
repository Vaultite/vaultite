## Summary

A methodological essay that asks ethnographers to study infrastructure, the boring things that sink into the background of practice. Star lists the properties that make something infrastructure and proposes ways of making it visible: reading its standards, its traces and the work that keeps it running.

## Notes

- Infrastructure is relational: it is infrastructure for someone, in some practice, and a barrier for someone else.
- Properties include embeddedness, transparency in use, reach beyond a single event, being learned as part of membership, embodying standards, being built on an installed base, and becoming visible on breakdown.
- Method: look at the master narratives built into systems, surface the invisible work, and treat the paradoxes of infrastructure as data.
- The essay is short and quotable; it works well as a first reading in a seminar.
