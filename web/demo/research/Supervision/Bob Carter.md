# Bob Carter

Doctoral student, second year. Thesis: Paul Otlet's documentation cards and the idea of a universal bibliography, 1895 to 1934. Co-supervised with the history department.

## Thesis plan

1. Introduction: documentation as a science of knowledge units
2. The card as standard: formats, sizes and the monographic principle [@otlet1934]
3. Classification in practice [@bowker1999]
4. Users and correspondents: the bibliography as a shared object [@star1989]
5. Afterlives: from the Mundaneum to hypertext [@bush1945; @nelson1965]
