# KNOW 214: Media of knowledge

Undergraduate seminar, spring term 2027. Tuesdays 14:00 to 16:00. Twelve weeks.

## Description

How do the tools we write with shape what we know? This course follows the media of scholarly knowledge from oral cultures and early writing to card indexes, hypertext and citation networks, asking at each step what a medium makes easy, what it makes hard, and who does the work of keeping it running.

## Assessment

- Weekly reading notes in a shared vault (30%)
- Short essay on one historical note system, 2,000 words (30%)
- Final project: a study of a note system in use, 4,000 words (40%)
