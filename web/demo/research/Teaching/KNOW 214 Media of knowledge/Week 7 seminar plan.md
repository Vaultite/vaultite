# Week 7 seminar plan

The card index as partner. Readings: [@luhmann1981] in translation and [@schmidt2016].

## Plan

1. Ten minutes: each student shows one card or note from their own vault and says why it is linked where it is.
2. Twenty minutes: Luhmann's four rules on the board (fixed numbers, branching, links, register). Which do our tools keep, and which do they drop?
3. Twenty minutes: is the index a partner? Bring in Otto's notebook [@clark1998] and ask what would have to be true.
4. Break.
5. Thirty minutes: Schmidt's archival picture against Luhmann's own account. What does the essay leave out?
6. Ten minutes: set up the short essay.

## Questions to hold back

- If the index surprises its author, who is the author of the surprise?
- Does a search box make the register obsolete, or does it change what counts as an entry point?
