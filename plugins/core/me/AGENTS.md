## Me (`ME.md`)
The user's own file, at the vault's top unless the setting `path` puts it elsewhere (a `type: me` file anywhere is
found too). Read it before writing anything: who the user is, the people who matter, how they want AIs to work with
them (how dictation mangles names, what not to store). Frontmatter `type: me`, `location` (where they live; the app
fills `coordinates`). Body: `## About me` (lasting facts), `## How to work with me` (preferences for AIs). Add a fact
with `people.remember` (`vau remember`), which puts it under the right heading once; never rewrite what's there.
