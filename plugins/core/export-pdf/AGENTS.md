## Export to PDF
"Export to PDF" (the palette, a note's menu) and "Print note" (the palette) print the note as it reads (its title, then its text with
images, embeds, blocks, math and code; no frontmatter), in light colours on white whatever the theme. The desktop app
asks where to save the PDF; a browser opens its print dialog, where "Save as PDF" makes one. Nothing is written to the
vault.
