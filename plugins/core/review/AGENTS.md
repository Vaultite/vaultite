against its text before the first unreviewed change (File history). `vau review` (`review.list`, `--by ChatGPT`) lists
them; keeping or reverting is the user's (`review.keep`, `review.revert` refuse agents). In the app: `view:review`, one
writer's `view:review/<name>`, one file's `view:review-file/<path>`.
