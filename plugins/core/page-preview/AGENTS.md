## Page preview
Pointing at a [[link]] for a moment (computers, not phones) shows that note in a popover: its text, blocks and
embeds, scrollable, with links you can click (and point at again, for a preview inside it); `[[Note#Heading]]` opens
scrolled to that heading, `[[Note#^id]]` shows that block. It works on links in notes, dashboards and text the app
draws, and on the files in the Links panel and in search results. Nothing is stored. `trigger: auto` (the default):
pointing is enough, but in a note being edited ⌘ must be held.
