## Now (`Now.md`)
The user's Now note, the twin of ME.md: ME.md is who they are, this is what they're on. At the vault's top unless the
setting `path` puts it elsewhere (a `type: now` file anywhere is found too); there's none until they make one. Read it
with ME.md; asked what to do next, answer from it with one thing. Frontmatter `type: now`. Body: `## Tracks`, at most
four, each a `### <Track>` with `- Why:`, `- Next:` (one action of 30 minutes or less) and `- Moved:` (the date and
what moved); then `## Promises`, a line for each thing an agent promised them (`- <date>: <what was promised>`), removed
once kept. Keep it current with `vau edit`, changing only the line that moved: a track's Next or Moved, a promise made
or kept. Never rewrite their tracks or add a fifth (a new one swaps one out, and which is their call), and keep it short.
