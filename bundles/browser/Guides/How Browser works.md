Browser makes the app a web browser first, with your notes one move away.

## Where things are
- **⌘T** (or New tab, the last row of Tabs) opens a bar: type an address, words to search the web, or the name of a note, a tab or a bookmark. Esc and nothing is opened.
- **Your favourites** are the tiles at the top of the left sidebar: pages you keep, web pages or notes. Right-click a tab, Pin, to add its page.
- **Tabs** are under them, top to bottom. Pinned tabs (right-click a tab, Pin tab) stay at the top. The others are today's: one you haven't looked at for 12 hours is archived, so the list stays short. Find it again in ⌘T, or in Archived tabs.
- **Spaces** are the numbers at the top of the sidebar: one for each part of your life, with its own tabs and favourites. Switch with ⌃1 to ⌃5 or a two-finger swipe over the sidebar; right-click one to give it an icon, a colour and a container.
- **Containers** keep logins apart, so you can be signed in to one site twice (Personal, Work). The button at the left of the address picks a page's.
- **Glance**: ⌥-click a link to look at it over the page, then open it or close it and carry on.
- **Links**, in the right sidebar, are your notes about the page you're on and the ones linking to it. "Note on this page" in the page's … menu starts one.
- **Agents** browse in tabs of their own, in the Agents container, beside yours and never in them. "Show agents in the browser" shows what they're doing.
- On your phone and in a web browser, web pages open in that device's browser, and Tabs shows your Mac's open pages live.

## Making it yours
Each part is a setting: how long tabs wait before they're archived (or never) in Tabs' settings, the tab bar and the colours in Appearance, the Inbox button from a right-click on the sidebar's header. Glass, in Get bundles, makes the window translucent. Settings, Restore previous setup puts your old setup back, and this guide is yours to edit or delete.
