Zen keeps only what you're working on on screen. Nothing is gone: it's all one move away.

## Finding things
- **The sidebars** hide until your pointer reaches the left or right edge of the window, then slide in, and the page dims softly behind them. Your pinned pages are there as tiles: point at one to see its name. Clicking a folder in the path above a note slides the sidebar in with it shown in Files.
- **Writing**: the path above the note fades while you type and comes back when you move the pointer. A note's properties stay out of its text: "Show properties in notes" in the command palette brings them back.
- **No tab bar.** Switch tabs from the Tabs panel in the left sidebar, or open anything by name with ⌘O.
- **Glance**: ⌥-click a link, a file in the sidebar or a search result to see it over the page instead of going there. Edit it in place, open it in a tab, or close it and carry on.
- Web pages open in a tab beside your notes (Web viewer).

## Making it yours
Each part is a setting: bring the tab bar back or turn off the fading in Settings, Appearance, the icon rails or the dimming in its Sidebars, or pin pages from a file's menu. Settings, Restore previous setup puts your old setup back, and this guide is yours to edit or delete.
