Zen keeps only what you're working on on screen. Nothing is gone: it's all one move away.

## Finding things
- **The sidebars** hide until your pointer reaches the left or right edge of the window, then slide in. Your pinned pages are there as tiles: point at one to see its name.
- **No tab bar.** Switch tabs from the Tabs panel in the left sidebar, or open anything by name with ⌘O.
- **Glance**: ⌥-click a link, a file in the sidebar or a search result to see it over the page instead of going there. Edit it in place, open it in a tab, or close it and carry on.
- Web pages open in a tab beside your notes (Web viewer).

## Making it yours
Each part is a setting: bring the tab bar or the icon rails back in Settings, Appearance, or pin pages from a file's menu. Settings, Restore previous setup puts your old setup back, and this guide is yours to edit or delete.
