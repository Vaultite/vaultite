# Lighthouse

A small app that reminds you to water your plants

---

## The problem

- Plants die quietly, a week after the last watering
- The apps that exist want an account and a subscription

---

## The idea

- One screen: every plant and when it needs water
- Works offline, no account
- A photo per plant, so you know which is which
