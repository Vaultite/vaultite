This is the **playground**: the vault of someone made up, so you can try Vaultite without touching your own. Every page here is drawn from plain Markdown files. Change anything: the playground starts fresh each time it opens.

## Talk to it

Vaultite is made for AIs to write in. [Open Claude Code](vaultite://command/terminal:claude-split) beside this page and try:

- "Give me a tour."
- "I had a burrito for lunch."
- "Called Bob for 20 minutes about the trip."

Each one writes a file, and the pages follow.
